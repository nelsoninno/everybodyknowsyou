The name

A celebration of recognition, wrapped in three words.

"Everybody knows you." It is what you say to someone bold. To someone famous. To someone people are looking up to. To someone who lives on their own terms. To someone who generates impact.

That is the promise of the service in three words: in three days, everybody knows you. The phrase doubles as a verdict and as a destination.

The logo

Two ideas in one mark.

Binoculars: the people looking at you

The icon is a pair of binoculars. Binoculars are the people looking at you. Customers, fans, journalists, your future boss, the AI tools searching for your name. The whole point of the service is to make sure that, when they look, they find you, accurately and at your best.

Asterisks: the eyes that mean bold

Inside each lens is an asterisk. The asterisks are the eyes. They are also a typographic mark. In Markdown, words wrapped in asterisks become bold. The icon says, in a single image: the people are looking at the bold.

The feel

Playful and impossible to ignore. Confident about the work we do.

The colors are loud on purpose. Lime grabs attention. Purple gives the brand its anchor. Pink is the smile. Together they are unapologetic and warm at the same time, which is also how we want every site we build for a client to feel. Behind the playfulness is real technical work, AI-native, structured, measurable, that we let our results speak for.
