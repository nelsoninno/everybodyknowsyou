What can I do so my business shows up in AI answers?

Start with four things. First, have your own website that says clearly what you do, for whom, and where. Second, use the same business name, address and links everywhere: your website, your Google Business Profile, your social profiles and directories. Third, answer the real questions your customers ask, in plain words, on your site. Fourth, collect proof, like reviews, press and client stories. AI repeats what it can check. If you want it done for you, that is our job.

How do I make my website findable by AI?

AI tools read websites a lot like search engines do. Make sure your pages load fast, your text is real text and not inside images, each page has one clear topic, and structured data says who you are (for example Organization, LocalBusiness, Person or FAQ). Check that your robots.txt does not block AI crawlers, and get trusted sites to link to you. Every EKY website ships with this Discoverable by AI package (SEO, GEO, LMO) from day one.

I want AI to talk about my brand. Where do I start?

Start by making it easy for AI to describe you in one sentence: who you are, what you do, who you do it for, and why you are different. If you cannot say it clearly, AI cannot either. Put that sentence on your website, then back it up with details and proof. This positioning work is part of every EKY project, and it often changes more than the website. How does my business show up in AI answers?

When someone asks an AI a question, it either searches the web right then or uses what it learned from the web before. Either way, it looks for sources that are relevant, clear and trustworthy, and it names the businesses those sources describe best. So you show up when your own website, and other sites, say the same clear things about you. You earn your place with information AI can read and check.

Why doesn't my business show up in Google AI answers?

The most common reasons we see: you have no website, or your website does not say clearly what you do and where. Your business details are different from one platform to another. Your content lives only on Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, where search engines see only part of it, and only for a short time. Your site is slow or built in a way crawlers cannot read. Or nobody else online mentions you yet. Our free audit shows which of these apply to you.

Why doesn't my business show up on Google at all?

Usually because Google cannot find a page that clearly belongs to you. Without your own website and a verified Google Business Profile, Google has very little to show. New websites also need time: Google has to discover, crawl and trust a site, and that takes weeks to months. We set everything up so that clock starts on day one.

Why don't ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude mention my business?

AI assistants recommend what they can find and check. If the information about you is thin, does not match across platforms, or is stuck inside social media posts, they pick a competitor they understand better, or a big generic company. We wrote a short article about this: Why doesn't my business show up in AI answers?

Is posting on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok enough to show up in AI answers?

It helps with people who already follow you, but it is not enough for AI. Posts fade within days, and most of what you post is hard for search engines and AI to index and find again later. A website keeps working for you every day. Keep posting, and also give AI a permanent source it can read.

How long does it take to show up in AI answers?

Your EKY website is live in 3 days, but being chosen by AI is earned over time. Searches for your own name often improve within weeks. Showing up for broader questions, like "best surf retreat in El Salvador", takes months of steady signals. Anyone who guarantees you a spot in AI answers is not being honest with you.

What is AI discoverability, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in a list of links. AI discoverability, also called AEO, GEO or LMO, helps you become the answer when someone asks an AI. They overlap, because both need a fast, clear and well-structured website. AI discoverability puts extra focus on clear facts, structured data, details that match across the web, and content that answers questions directly. We do both.
