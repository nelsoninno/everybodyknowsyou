What happens next

Annual renewal

The first year is included. After that, your site renews for $29 a year, which covers your domain, hosting, and keeping your site online.

Premium domains: some special domains (short names or premium extensions) cost more. Those are billed at cost, whatever the registrar charges, on top of the $29.

Changes after launch

While we build, we include several design iterations (2 in Personal, 3 in Business, 10 in Executive Flash) to get your site just right. Once it's live, any new change is paid; pick what suits you:

  • A one-off change$15
